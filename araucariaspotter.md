<p>Araucaria araucana is a living fossil: a tree whose ancestors stood among dinosaurs, with
reptile-like leaves and a sharp spiral crown. It is endangered in the wild, where it grows only in
Chile and Argentina — and yet, planted, it turns up in parks and front gardens across Europe and
beyond. Araucaria Spotter is a free, community-made map of where it has ended up. People record the
trees they find; the map shows where the species has travelled.</p>
